About Us

The dirty work, handled with a smile.

Piles Be Gone started with a simple idea: nobody actually enjoys scooping after their dog — so let someone friendly and dependable take it off your plate. Owner Sebby Rocco Abbate keeps it simple, tidy, and on schedule.

We're a local Clay business that treats your yard with respect. We clean up thoroughly, bag and remove the waste, and leave a closed gate and a lawn the whole family (two-legged and four-legged) can enjoy.
