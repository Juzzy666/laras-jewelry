About Baxter

Local hands that take pride in the details.

Baxter Lawncare is a Canastota-based lawn care and landscaping service owned and operated by Kenneth E. Baxter. We treat every lawn like it's our own — showing up when we say we will and leaving each property looking sharp.

From weekly mowing to seasonal cleanups and fresh mulch beds, we handle the work that keeps your yard healthy and your weekends free. No giant crews, no runaround — just careful, dependable work close to home.

Our Services

Everything your lawn needs.

Pick a one-time job or set up a seasonal plan — we'll keep your property looking its best.

Mowing & Maintenance

Reliable weekly or bi-weekly mowing with clean edges and trimmed lines that keep your lawn looking crisp all season. Set it and forget it — we'll be there.

Spring & Fall Cleanups

Leaf removal, bed clearing, and debris cleanup to get your yard ready for the season ahead.

Mulching & Beds

Fresh mulch, defined edges, and tidy planting beds that make your landscaping pop and keep weeds down.

Trimming & Hedges

Shrub shaping, hedge trimming, and overgrowth cleanup to keep your borders neat and well-kept.

Seasonal Plans
