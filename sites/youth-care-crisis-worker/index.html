About

A caring professional in your corner.

Leartis Carswell Jr. works alongside young people and their families in the Syracuse community, bringing a calm, respectful presence to the moments that feel hard. The focus is simple: build trust, listen well, and help young people find their footing.

Every young person deserves a steady adult who believes in them. This work is grounded in patience, dignity, and the belief that with the right support, young people can grow into their strengths.

How We Help

Support built around the young person.

Caring, professional support focused on mentorship, stability, and growth — always at a young person's own pace.

Youth Mentorship

One-to-one mentorship that builds trust over time — a consistent, encouraging adult who shows up and listens.

Crisis Support & De-escalation

A calm, grounding presence in difficult moments — helping young people feel safe, steady, and supported as things settle.

Family Support

Working alongside parents and caregivers with respect and care, so the whole family can move forward together.

School & Community Collaboration

Partnering with schools, programs, and community organizations to surround young people with consistent, connected support.

Skill-Building

Helping young people grow practical, everyday skills — communication, coping, and confidence — that support a stronger future.
