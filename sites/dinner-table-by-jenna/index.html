The Maker

A small-batch maker at heart.

Dinner Table by Jenna began at a kitchen table in Syracuse — the place where the best conversations happen. Every piece is made by hand in small batches, with an eye for warmth, texture, and the little details that make a home feel lived-in and loved.

I make things meant to be used: gathered around, passed across, and brought out for the moments that matter. No two are exactly alike, and I think that's what makes them feel like yours.
