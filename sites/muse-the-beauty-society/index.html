The Studio

An invitation to slow down.

MUSE The Beauty Society was created by Alexis M. Brown to be more than a treatment room — it's a society of self-care. A place in Syracuse where every visit is unhurried, every detail considered, and every guest leaves feeling like the most refined version of themselves.
