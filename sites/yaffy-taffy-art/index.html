Hi, I'm Jessica

A CNY maker & illustrator.

Yaffy Taffy Art is my little Syracuse studio, where every piece starts with a doodle and ends up somewhere joyful. I work in bright palettes and playful shapes — the kind of art that makes a room (and a face) light up.

Whether it's an original painting, a printed design, or a one-of-a-kind commission dreamed up just for you, I make everything by hand, in small batches, with a whole lot of heart.
