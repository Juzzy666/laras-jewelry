Who We Are

Local, owner-operated, hands-on.

Elevate Services is a Camillus-based general contracting business run by owner John Hayward Gardner Jr. When you hire us, you work directly with the person doing the work — not a sales desk or a sub-out shuffle.

We take on residential remodels and improvement projects of all sizes, treating every job site like it's our own home. The goal is simple: quality work, on a schedule you can count on, with no surprises.
