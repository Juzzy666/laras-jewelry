The Collector

Built in the 315, for the 315.

315 Syracuse Singles started the way most collections do — one card at a time. Owner Justin Miguel Rivera turned a lifelong love of the hobby into a place where Central New York collectors can find the singles they're chasing.

No mystery, no pressure — just an honest collector who knows the difference a clean centering or the right rookie can make. If you're hunting for that one card, you're in the right area code.
