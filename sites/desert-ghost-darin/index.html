The Maker

Jewelry with soul. Fashion with a story.

Desert Ghost is the new fashion label from Michelle DaRin — a metalsmith and artist who has spent her career making bold, handmade pieces that don't look like anyone else's. Each design is one of a kind, built from forged and enameled copper, cast silver, resin, bronze, leather, feathers, and gemstones.

Her latest chapter reaches beyond jewelry: a sustainability-minded fashion line that reimagines vintage scarves, rugs, and fabrics into wearable art — '70s-inspired, free-spirited, and unmistakably her own.

Michelle holds a B.A. in Sculpture from SUNY Cortland and an M.F.A. in Metalsmithing from Syracuse University, where she also teaches. She works from a studio built on family land in Jamesville, NY — within earshot of a babbling brook.
