Family Owned · 315

Where fire meets flavor.

Fire & Ice 315 is a locally owned, family-run food truck built on one simple idea: bold, satisfying street food made fresh when you order it. No shortcuts, just good eats served with a smile.

From juicy spiedies to loaded handhelds, our menu brings the heat (and the cool) to events, lots, and gatherings all over Central New York. Follow along to see where we're parked next.
