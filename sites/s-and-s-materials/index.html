Our Story

A family operation, rooted in Eaton.

S&S Materials is owned and run by Shawn & Shana Payne, a family supplier based in Eaton, New York. We grew out of working the land ourselves, and that's still how we do business — honest weights, quality material, and a handshake you can trust.

From a few yards of mulch for the garden beds to loads of stone and fill for a big project, we'll help you figure out what you need and get it where it's going. Local, family-run, and proud of it.
