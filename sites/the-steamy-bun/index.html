Our Story

A family table, opened to Syracuse.

The Steamy Bun started where so many good things do — around a family table. Jacqueline and Michael Paul Parks wanted to share the food they love most: warm, fresh, made-by-hand comfort that fills the room with steam and the kind of smell that pulls you to the kitchen.

Everything is cooked to order, the way you'd make it at home if you had all afternoon. Soft buns, hearty plates, and a welcome that feels like Sunday dinner — that's the whole idea.

Fresh from our kitchen to your table.
