Our Mission

Creativity, care, and hope.

Art That Heals Ministry began with a simple belief: that making art can comfort, connect, and uplift. Founded by Alice Denise Young in Syracuse, our ministry creates gentle, welcoming spaces where kids and families can express themselves and feel seen.

Rooted in faith and open to all, we pair creativity with kindness — offering programs and gatherings that nurture young hearts and build community, one project at a time.

"A joyful heart is good medicine." — A guiding spirit behind everything we do.

Where creativity meets compassion.
