Our Story

A little kitchen, a lot of love.

Butter & Bloom is a home-grown Brewerton studio where the oven and the flower bench sit side by side. Every cake, cookie, and bouquet is made by hand, in small batches, with an eye for the details that make a table feel special.

We pair what we bake with what we arrange — so the dessert and the flowers feel like they belong to the same celebration. No two orders are ever quite the same, and that's exactly the point.
