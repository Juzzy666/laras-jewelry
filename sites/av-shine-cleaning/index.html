About AV Shine

Cleaning you can count on.

AV Shine Cleaning is a Syracuse-based cleaning service owned and run by Alona Rusanova. We serve both homes and workplaces, bringing the same careful, consistent standard to every space we touch.

We believe a clean space should be easy to keep up. That's why we listen first, build the right plan for your space, and show up ready to make it shine — whether it's a one-time refresh or an ongoing routine.

What you can expect

  • A thorough, top-to-bottom clean every visit
  • Friendly, dependable, on-time service
  • A plan tailored to your home or office
  • Eco-friendly product options on request
Our Services

Cleaning for every space.

From a single deep clean to a steady weekly routine, here's how we keep things shining.

Standard Cleaning

Regular upkeep of the spaces you use most — dusting, floors, kitchens, and baths kept fresh and tidy.

Deep Cleaning

Office Cleaning
