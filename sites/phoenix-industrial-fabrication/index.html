The Shop

Hands-on craft, heavy-duty results.

Phoenix Industrial Fabrication is a Syracuse-based welding and metal fabrication shop run by Harold D. Cericola Jr. We take on the jobs that need real skill behind the torch — from one-off custom builds to structural work that has to hold.

Every weld is laid by hand and every project gets the same straight-talk approach: clean fit-up, solid joints, and finished work you can count on. If it's made of metal, there's a good chance we can build it, weld it, or bring it back to life.

What We Do

Fabrication & welding services.

Custom work for contractors, businesses, farms, and homeowners across Central New York.

Custom Fabrication

One-off and short-run metal parts, brackets, frames, and assemblies cut, formed, and welded to your specs.

Structural & MIG/TIG Welding

Structural steel, mild steel, stainless, and aluminum joined with clean MIG and TIG welds built to carry the load.

Repairs & Reinforcement

Cracked frames, broken equipment, worn brackets — we patch, reinforce, and rebuild metal that's seen better days.

Railings & Stairs

Custom steel railings, handrails, and stair stringers for shops, decks, and entryways — sturdy and made to fit.

On-Site Work

Portable welding and field repairs brought to your job site, shop floor, or property when the work can't come to us.

Project Consults

Got an idea or a problem to solve? Bring the sketch or the broken part — we'll talk through the best way to build it.

Built to last.

Whether it's a fresh build or a repair that has to hold up, Phoenix puts in the work so your metal outlasts the job.
