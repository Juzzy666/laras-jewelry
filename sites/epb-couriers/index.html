About EPB

Two owners, one mission: get it there.

EPB Couriers is a family-owned delivery business founded by Eddie L. Brown and Porscha Brown. We started it to give Syracuse-area businesses and neighbors a courier they can actually count on — responsive, careful, and quick.

Because we're owner-run, your delivery is handled by people who treat every drop-off like it matters. From a single urgent envelope to a recurring route, we move it with care and keep you in the loop the whole way.
