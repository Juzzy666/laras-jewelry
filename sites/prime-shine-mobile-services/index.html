About Prime Shine

Detailing built around you.

Prime Shine Mobile Services is a Syracuse-based mobile detailing business owned by Otis L. Tillie. The whole idea is convenience without compromise: pro-level results delivered wherever your vehicle is parked.

We treat every car, truck, and SUV like it's our own — careful products, the right technique, and an obsessive eye for the finish. Whether it's a quick refresh or a full transformation, we bring the gear and the polish to you.
