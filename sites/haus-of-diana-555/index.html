The Story

A little haus with a lot of character.

Haus of Diana 555 is a new boutique in Canastota born from a love of beautiful, unexpected things. Owner Roseanna Gherica Bates curates each corner of the shop by hand — mixing the timeless with the surprising, the everyday with the special.

Step inside and you'll find an ever-changing collection chosen with care. Nothing mass-feeling, nothing predictable — just pieces meant to be discovered, and made to be loved.
