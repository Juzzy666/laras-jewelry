In the Workshop

Good bones deserve a second life.

Turn Tables is a small furniture restoration and refinishing studio in Auburn, New York, run by Cynthia Wilder. Every piece that comes through the shop gets the same patient attention — stripped back, repaired, and finished by hand.

Some pieces are brought back to their original character; others are reimagined with new color and style. Either way, the goal is the same: take something well-made and worn, and give it a story worth keeping.
