About the Shop

Hydraulic know-how, close to home.

Stone Quarry Hydraulics is a Cazenovia-based hydraulics and industrial repair shop run by Gary Robert Bushneck. When a cylinder blows, a hose lets go, or a piece of equipment quits under load, we get it diagnosed and back in service.

We work on the gear that keeps our area running — tractors, loaders, excavators, dump trailers, and shop machinery. Solid repairs, custom-built assemblies, and straight answers about what your equipment really needs.

What We Do

Hydraulic service & repair.

Repair, fabrication, and field service for farm, construction, and industrial equipment.

01

Cylinder Repair & Rebuild

Reseal, hone, and rebuild hydraulic cylinders — new seals, rods, and packing to bring them back to spec and stop the leaks.

02

Hose Assemblies

Custom hydraulic hoses built and crimped to length while you wait, with the right fittings for your machine and pressure.

03

Custom Fabrication

Brackets, mounts, manifolds, and adapters fabricated to fit when an off-the-shelf part doesn't exist or won't do.

04

On-Site Field Service

Mobile repairs brought to your farm or job site when equipment is down and there's no time to haul it in.

05

Parts & Components

Seals, fittings, couplers, and hydraulic components sourced and supplied to keep your repair moving.
