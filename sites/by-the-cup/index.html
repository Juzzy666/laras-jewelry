Our Scoop

Little cups, big smiles.

By The Cup is a brand-new Liverpool sweet spot built on a simple idea: a good scoop can turn any ordinary day around. We keep it cheerful, we keep it generous, and we keep it made-fresh.

Whether you're chasing a cone after a game, cooling off on a warm walk, or just treating yourself because you can — there's always room for a little happiness, served by the cup.
